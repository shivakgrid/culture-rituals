# Global Ancestor Traditions

The human instinct to honor ancestors is universal. Many global cultures feature a specific time of year when the boundary between realms is believed to thin, almost always tied to seasonal transitions.

| Tradition | Culture | Timeline | Core Similarities to Pitru Paksha |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Día de los Muertos** | Mexico & Latin America | Oct 31 – Nov 2 | Families build altars (*ofrendas*) offering the deceased's favorite foods and water, welcoming spirits for a joyous reunion. |
| **Obon (Bon Festival)** | Japan | Mid-August | Families clean gravesites, offer food, and light lanterns to guide visiting ancestor spirits, mirroring the intent of *Tarpan*. |
| **Zhongyuan** | China & East Asia | August/September | Elaborate vegetarian meals and incense are offered to ancestors and wandering spirits, sharing the concept of universal charity (*Annadan*). |
| **Chuseok** | South Korea | September/October | A harvest festival where freshly harvested rice and traditional foods are offered to ancestors in gratitude for the season's bounty. |
| **Samhain / All Souls' Day** | Celtic / Christian | Oct 31 – Nov 2 | A period to offer prayers to help departed souls find peace, directly analogous to aiding the ancestors' spiritual journey. |
