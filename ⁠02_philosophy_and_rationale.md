⁠# Philosophy and Rationale

Pitru Paksha is not rooted in superstition or fear; it is a structured ethical duty centered on gratitude, legacy, and the cycle of karma.

## The Three Debts (Rinas)
Every individual is born with fundamental philosophical debts:
1.  **Deva Rina:** Debt to the divine and natural forces.
2.  **Rishi Rina:** Debt to teachers and knowledge givers.
3.  **Pitru Rina:** Debt to parents, ancestors, and lineage.

## The Logical Framework of the Rituals
*   **Genetic Gratitude:** Our physical health, cultural upbringing, and opportunities stem from previous generations. This fortnight is an annual reminder to express conscious gratitude for this inheritance.
*   **Grief Work and Psychological Closure:** The structured rites give living family members a dedicated avenue to process lingering grief, recall departed loved ones, and offer peaceful intent toward their memory.
*   **Ecological Consciousness:** Rituals like *Annadan* (feeding animals and the community) channel private ancestral remembrance into public social and ecological good.
*   **Symbolism over Superstition:** Offering water (the fundamental element of life) and sesame seeds (representing resilience) are symbolic acts of providing enduring peace to an ancestor's memory, rather than arbitrary magic.
